## Severity Guide

- `P0`: changes the perceived product category or trust/legal status
- `P1`: blocks a likely visitor from trying the product
- `P2`: creates friction but does not block understanding
- `P3`: polish issue or minor ambiguity

## Status Guide

- `open`: still appears in the latest review
- `improved`: less frequent or less severe, but still present
- `resolved`: no longer appears in panel responses
- `accepted`: true limitation or intentionally deferred message
